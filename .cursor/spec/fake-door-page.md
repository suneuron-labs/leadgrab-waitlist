### Prompt for Cursor to Build the Landing Page

Create a clean project folder in Cursor (using Next.js  + Tailwind or single-file HTML  + Tailwind) and run this prompt:

Create a modern, high-converting SaaS landing page for a Chrome Extension called "WA LeadGrab".

Use Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide React icons.

Page Structure:

1 . Navigation Bar:

    - Logo: "WA LeadGrab" with a modern green chat-sheet icon.

    - Links: Features, How It Works, Pricing, FAQ.

    - Header CTA Button: "Get Early Access"

2 . Hero Section:

    - Pill Badge: "Built for Sales Reps, Recruiters & Community Managers"

    - H1 Headline: "Export WhatsApp Groups & Contacts to Clean Excel in 1 Click"

    - Subheading: "Stop copying phone numbers one by one. Grab group members, unsaved numbers, and business chats with pristine formatting, proper country codes, and zero scientific notation errors."

    - Primary CTA Button: " [ Add to Chrome  - Free Beta  ]" (WhatsApp emerald green)

    - Trust Subtext: "⭐ Free 50-contact trial • In-browser privacy (no data stored) • Manifest V3 safe"

    - Hero Mockup: A clean dashboard preview showing WhatsApp Web on the left and a floating extension drawer on the right displaying "428 contacts detected", selective checkmarks, and an "Export to Excel" button.

3 . The "Why Us" Pain-Point Comparison (Grid):

    - Card 1: "No More Scrambled Numbers" (Explains auto country-code formatting without Excel scientific notation bugs).

    - Card 2: "Selective Pre-Export Filtering" (Explains 1-click admin exclusion and custom member checkmarks).

    - Card 3: "Always Up-to-Date" (Highlights 24h hotfix commitment for WhatsApp Web updates).

4 . Interactive Feature Showcase:

    - Demonstrate the difference between a raw contact dump vs. WA LeadGrab's organized table (Name, Clean Phone, Country Code, Role).

5 . Transparent Pricing Table:

    - Card 1: "Free Trial" ($0)  - > First 50 contacts lifetime, standard CSV export, basic formatting.

    - Card 2: "Pro Monthly" ($12/mo)  - > Unlimited exports, selective checkmarks, admin exclusion, Excel (.xlsx) & vCard support, priority hotfixes.

    - Card 3: "Pro Annual" ($89/yr)  - > Everything in Pro with 38% annual discount.

6 . Private Beta / Early Access Modal (The Validation Trap):

    - Clicking any "Add to Chrome" or "Get Access" button opens a polished modal.

    - Modal Title: "Join the Next WA LeadGrab Batch"

    - Modal Copy: "We are onboarding users in batches of 50 to maintain stability with recent WhatsApp Web releases. Enter your email to receive your access link and Chrome Store invite."

    - Form: Work email input  + "Request Beta Invite" button. Store submissions in local state or log to console.

Design Style: Clean, enterprise-grade dark/light contrast, Emerald Green ( #10B981) accents, sharp typography, generous whitespace, fully mobile responsive.
